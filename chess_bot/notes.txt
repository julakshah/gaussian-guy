ModifiedKinSpace()
  Computes final joint angles

  Location:
    widowx_controller.py

  Where is it Used:
    set_ee_pose_matrix_fast() within widowx_controller.py

  Returns:
    list of final thetas for each end effector
      - [joint_1_angle, joint_2angle, ...]
    Boolean for whether it succeeded 
      - True is solution found

  Arguments:
    Slist - IDK - self.robot_des.Slist
    M - IDK - self.robot_des.M
    T - IDK - T_sd
    thetalist0 - Guess for joint angles
    eomg - IDK - 0.001
    ev - IDK - 0.001
    maxiterations=40
